Support

Updated October 24, 2026

Need a hand? Email support@ascendhealthos.app. Ascend is made by a very small team, so replies usually take one to two business days. Telling us your iPhone model and iOS version helps a lot. You can also write to us from inside the app: Profile → Send feedback.

What Ascend does

Type what you're craving the way you'd say it — a dish, a place, a mood — and Ascend fits it to your goal: a recipe you can make, with every change explained; what to order at a restaurant nearby, step by step; or, if you've already eaten, one line like "I had a chicken sandwich and waffle fries" and it's logged with the restaurant's published numbers. Today's targets sit on your Home screen and update as you log, and a Home Screen widget shows your next meal.

Ascend+ — the subscription

You can use Ascend free. Ascend+ is the optional subscription, monthly or yearly; the yearly plan starts with a free trial. Prices and the trial's length are shown on the Ascend+ screen in your own currency, as set by the App Store for your region. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when the trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Managing or cancelling

In Ascend: Profile → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Ascend+. Cancelling stops the renewal; you keep Ascend+ until the end of the period you've paid for. Apple handles subscriptions, so we can't cancel one on your behalf.

Refunds

All purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid but Ascend+ isn't showing

Open the Ascend+ screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the date of purchase.

Your account and your data

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in (with Apple, Google or an email code) is optional, from Profile.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account. This removes your account from our servers. It does not cancel a subscription — do that in Manage subscription first.

How do I erase everything on my phone?

Profile → Reset the app. This erases your log, weight, photos, targets and saved meals from the device.

What leaves your phone and why is set out in the privacy policy.

Common questions

Where do the numbers come from?

From the USDA's FoodData Central and restaurants' own published nutrition — never guessed. If a number looks wrong, email us what you typed, what you expected and what you saw.

Is Ascend medical advice?

No. Ascend is a general nutrition app, not a medical device, and nothing in it is a diagnosis or a treatment plan. Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before making significant changes, especially if you are pregnant, managing a health condition, or taking medication.

Is there an Android or web version?

Not today. Ascend is iPhone only.

Reporting a problem

The most useful report says what you did, what you expected, what happened instead, and includes a screenshot. Send it to support@ascendhealthos.app.
