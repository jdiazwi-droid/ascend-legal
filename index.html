Ascend — AI Health OS

Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

Ascend ("the app") is a personal health and fitness app. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and what never leaves your phone. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version: Ascend has no accounts and no sign-in. Your health data — meals, workouts, weight, check-ins, scores — lives on your device and stays there. The one exception is the coach: when you send the coach a message, that message and a summary of your day's plan are sent to our server and passed to OpenAI to generate the reply. Nothing is sold, nothing is used for advertising, and nothing identifies you personally.

Data stored on your device

Everything you put into Ascend is stored locally on your phone: your onboarding answers (goal, meals, training schedule, foods), daily plans and check-offs, hunger and energy check-ins, logged foods, workouts and routines, weight entries, your Health Score history, imported health data files, and your conversations with the coach. None of this is uploaded, synced, or backed up by us. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. The in-app "Reset the app" option (Settings) erases it all in place.

Data sent when you talk to the coach

To answer you, the app sends to our server (hosted on Supabase) and onward to OpenAI:

  • your message, and the conversation so far in that chat;
  • a summary of your day as the app understands it: your plan's meals, snacks, and workout (with completion state), portion and macro numbers, recent check-in answers, your weight trend, logged foods, and short traces of your recent coach conversations;
  • a random per-install identifier, used only to rate-limit requests. It is generated on your device, identifies the installation rather than you, and is reset when you reset or reinstall the app.

This data is used only to generate the coach's reply and to protect the service from abuse. We do not build profiles from it, sell it, or use it for advertising. OpenAI processes it as a service provider to generate the response; per OpenAI's API terms, API data is not used to train their models. The app asks for your explicit consent before your first coach message, and the coach does not work without it.

Microphone and speech

Voice typing uses your phone's microphone only while you are speaking to the coach, and speech is converted to text on your device by the operating system. Audio is never sent to us. The words appear in your text box, and nothing reaches the coach unless you press send.

Camera

The food scanner uses your camera two ways. Barcode and nutrition-label reading happens on your device; those images are never uploaded. If you choose to scan a meal photo, that photo is sent to our server and to OpenAI to identify the food and estimate portions, then discarded — it is not stored by us, and it is covered by the same in-app AI consent as the coach. Nothing is photographed or sent without you pressing the button.

Purchases

Ascend+ subscriptions are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, our subscription service provider. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and your purchase state so the app knows a subscription is active — nothing about your health.

What we don't do

No accounts. No analytics or tracking SDKs. No advertising. No selling or sharing of personal data. No use of your data to train AI models. No access to your Apple Health data (Ascend reads only files you explicitly import).

Children

Ascend is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, or use Settings → "Reset the app". Because we keep no copies of your device data, there is nothing further to request from us. Chat requests are processed transiently to generate replies and are subject to the retention policies of our service providers (Supabase, OpenAI, RevenueCat).
